#  Smart Campus Parking Platform — APM Product Strategy

> **End-to-End Product Strategy, PRD, Roadmap & AI Co-pilot Integration**  
> *Developed as an Associate Product Manager (APM) Portfolio Project.*

---

##  Project Overview
The **Smart Campus Parking Platform** is a smart mobility solution designed to solve campus traffic congestion, reduce average parking search time from **30 minutes to under 5 minutes**, and streamline enforcement across **4,000 parking spots in 8 campus zones**.

---

##  Target User Personas
* **Student Persona:** Quick parking access to avoid missing classes (Search time < 5 mins).
* **Faculty Member Persona:** Guaranteed reserved spots without unauthorized parking (95% availability).
* **Security Personnel Persona:** On-ground control and automated violation ticketing (< 90 seconds per ticket).
